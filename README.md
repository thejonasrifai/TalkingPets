3-Minute Video Script & Production Guide: "The Secret Life of Apartment Pets"

Genre: Comedy / Pet Mockumentary

Target Audience: Pet owners, animal lovers, comedy fans

Estimated Duration: 3 minutes

Tone: Sarcastic, dramatic, lighthearted

Act I: The Morning Struggle (0:00 - 1:00)

Scene 1: The Alarmer (0:00 - 0:30)

Visual: Close-up of a cat or dog staring blankly into the camera lens with deadpan eyes while sitting on a pillow. Slow zoom in.

Sound Effect: Loud, obnoxious smartphone alarm ringing, followed by an immediate click as it's snoozed.

Pet Voiceover (Deep, dramatic human voice):

"Day 1,420 of house arrest. The alarm screams. The tall ones stumble out of bed like zombies searching for sacred black bean juice. Do they acknowledge my existence? Negative. I am merely a decorative throw pillow to them."

Scene 2: Breakfast Injustice (0:30 - 1:00)

Visual: Footage of the pet walking over to an empty or half-full food bowl, sniffing it disdainfully, and looking up at the owner with profound betrayal.

Sound Effect: A tragic violin scratch or sad trombone note.

Pet Voiceover:

"Look at this. Look at it. I can see the bottom of the ceramic. This isn't a meal, this is a cruel psychological experiment. If I don't receive crunchies within the next 120 seconds, I will legally have to turn this house upside down."

Act II: The Great Outdoors & The Invader (1:00 - 2:15)

Scene 3: Window Watchers (1:00 - 1:35)

Visual: Pet staring intensely out the living room window, ears perked up, tracking a leaf or a passing delivery truck.

Sound Effect: Tense spy-thriller electronic synth pulse.

Pet Voiceover:

"Ah, the mail carrier. My mortal enemy. He thinks he can slide a piece of paper through the front slot unpunished? Not on my watch. Stand back, human—I am about to bark with the fury of a thousand suns."

Scene 4: The Unseen Threat (1:35 - 2:15)

Visual: Pet suddenly freezing, staring intensely at an empty corner of the ceiling or a speck of dust on the wall.

Sound Effect: High-pitched sci-fi UFO hum.

Pet Voiceover:

"Wait... nobody move. Do you see that speck of dust floating in Sector 4? It’s communicating with the shadow demons. I must hiss at the air. It’s the only way to keep us safe."

Action Cue: Pet does a sudden burst of frantic running (zoomies) across the rug, then instantly plops down and acts innocent.

Act III: The Afternoon Slump & Outro (2:15 - 3:00)

Scene 5: Exhausting Schedule (2:15 - 2:45)

Visual: Pet sleeping in a completely ridiculous, uncomfortable-looking pretzel shape on the couch.

Sound Effect: Cozy, relaxing acoustic guitar or lo-fi beat fading in.

Pet Voiceover:

"Phew. Protecting this household from mailmen, ghosts, and empty bowls is back-breaking labor. I’ve earned my 14th nap of the day. Do not wake me unless cheese is being unwrapped."

Scene 6: Call to Action (2:45 - 3:00)

Visual: Text overlay on screen while the pet yawns widely.

On-Screen Text & Voiceover:

"Does your pet think they run the house too? Drop their funniest moments in the comments, and hit subscribe for more daily pet drama!"

Editing & Production Checklist

Dynamic Subtitles: Use bold, colorful text tracking word-for-word for social media retention.

Timing: Leave a 1-second pause after every dramatic statement to let the pet's facial expressions sink in.

Sound Design: Heavy emphasis on comedic sound effects (gongs, sighs, dramatic swells) to sell the mockumentary vibe.
